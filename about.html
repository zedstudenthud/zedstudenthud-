🇿🇲 About Zed Student Hub

A platform created to help Zambian students learn, prepare and succeed.

🎓

Our Purpose

Zed Student Hub is a student-focused platform designed to bring useful educational resources and opportunities together in one convenient place.

Instead of students having to search through different places for learning resources, Zed Student Hub aims to make useful materials easier to discover and access.


📚

What We Provide

  • Study materials for Primary students
  • Study materials for Secondary students
  • University study resources
  • Past examination papers
  • Scholarship opportunities
  • Helpful academic resources

🚀

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a useful and accessible digital platform that supports students across Zambia in their education journey.

We want Zed Student Hub to become a place where students can find resources that help them study smarter, prepare better and work towards their academic goals.


🏆

Learn. Prepare. Succeed.

Education is a journey. Every lesson learned, every question answered and every examination prepared for is another step towards success.

Zed Student Hub is here to support that journey.
