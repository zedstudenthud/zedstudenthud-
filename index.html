STEM / ICT

Airtel Tech Fellowship

Airtel Zambia has launched 50 full scholarships for undergraduate students studying ICT and STEM programmes at UNZA and Copperbelt University.

What is included:

Tuition fees, accommodation, laptop, mobile data, monthly student living allowance, mentorship and career development opportunities.

Institutions: UNZA and CBU

Deadline: 10 October 2026

Apply / Details

Government Funding

HELSB Student Loans

The Higher Education Loans and Scholarships Board provides financial support to eligible Zambian students entering supported public universities.

Support can include tuition, accommodation and student allowances depending on the level awarded.

Eligibility includes: Zambian citizenship, admission to a supported institution, age and academic requirements, and demonstrated financial need.

Check the official portal for current deadlines.

Visit HELSB Portal

Source: Higher Education Loans & Scholarships Board

🔧
Skills Training

TEVET Bursary

The Ministry of Technology and Science TEVET Bursary supports vulnerable students who cannot afford user fees for skills training.

Students can use the official system to find participating institutions and courses, apply for a bursary and track applications.

Suitable for: Students pursuing technical, vocational and entrepreneurship training.

Visit TEVET Bursary

Source: Ministry of Technology and Science / TEVETA
